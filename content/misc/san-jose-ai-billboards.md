I took these photos while I was in San Jose for Open Sauce 2025. You may use them
according to the terms of the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Why did I take these photos? Mostly because I was surprised by the sheer amount
of advertising for AI startups in the Bay Area. It felt like every other billboard
was an advertisement for something AI related.
