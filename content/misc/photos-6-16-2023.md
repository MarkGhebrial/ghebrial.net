# Curtiss Wright R-1820 Cyclone

The museum had a cutaway R-1820 radial engine on display. I took these pictures because I found the inner workings of the engine to be quite nice to look at.

![](/images/air-and-space/curtis1.JPG)

![](/images/air-and-space/curtis2.JPG)

This one is my new desktop wallpaper.
![](/images/air-and-space/curtis3.JPG)

Here you can see the cams and rollers used for valve timing.
![](/images/air-and-space/curtis4.JPG)

This photo shows the push rod that the roller in the previous photo is connected to. Normally, the push rods are covered by those black tubes you can see next to the cylinders on the rest of the engine.
![](/images/air-and-space/curtis5.JPG)
