Mailroom is an email server designed for simplicity and quick setup.
It's still a work in progress and is not near completion.

<!-- {# Mailroom is written in Rust, using tokio as the async backend and sea-orm as the orm. #} -->

## Why?

Email, despite being a cornerstone internet technology, used to be a complete black box to me.
From the outside it seemed very complex and intimidating, partially because the relevant wikipedia articles use obtuse acronyms like MTA, MUA, and MSA.


I did not have the slightest understanding of how it worked.
