## My cameras

As of August 2025, I have two cameras: A [Nikon D5600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikon_D5600) and a [Nikon FM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikon_FM). The D5600
is a pretty solid entry level APS-C DSLR. Paired with the kit lens, it's a lightweight
setup that's great for traveling.

The FM is a 35mm film SLR from the 70's. It's a very heavy and well built camera,
but is slightly uncomfortable to hold due to the lack of scooped grip. I may attempt
to design a grip for it in the future.
