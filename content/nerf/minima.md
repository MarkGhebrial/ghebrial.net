### Minima

![A picture of Minima](/minima.webp)

Minima is a blaster that I designed and prototyped myself, from scratch!
It's currently on its third (and a half) iteration and will be released as open source once I'm fully satisfied with it.

I designed Minima because I needed a reliable and simple sub 160fps springer.
I've definitely achieved that goal: Minima is (in my *totally* unbiased opinion) the best blaster in that niche.

I designed the blaster parametrically in Fusion 360.
I drew design inspiration from the Caliburn, [Lynx](https://orionblasters.com/), and [Skewer](https://github.com/CharamileDesigns/CX_Skewer), aiming to capture the minimalism of the Lynx, the aesthetic cohesiveness of the Skewer, and the foolproof mechanisms of the Caliburn.

Minima has been under development for over a year at this point, so why haven't I released it yet?
Well, there's still some things I'm not happy with:
- One of my main design goals was to have quick spring and barrel swaps.
  Currently, installing a stock mounting point eliminates the quick spring swap capability.
  Having a stock is pretty important for comfortably running any spring load heavier than a 788, so I won't release the blaster until I can figure out how to add a stock mount while preserving spring swapability.
- There's currently no easy way to adjust the tension of the sear elastic. I already have a solution in mind though.
